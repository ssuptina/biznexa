Business owners must approve definitions, periods, exclusions, and targets before the metrics become production measures.

## Reporting rules

- The UI labels the connected source as a **validated synthetic dataset** and shows its source period. It does not show fabricated KPI values when the API is unavailable.
- Financial ratios must be recalculated from their numerators and denominators after aggregation.
- Inventory value and customer-risk segments are snapshot measures. A historical trend requires consistent snapshots and point-in-time rules.
- Currency, fiscal calendar, reporting time zone, returns, cancellations, tax, shipping, and cut-off rules require business approval.
- Gross profit, contribution margin, and operating profit are distinct. Operating profit requires operating expense records and an approved accounting mapping.

## Customer Analytics implementation definitions

These are explicit operational definitions used by the customer analytics API. The dataset is synthetic, and these definitions should be reviewed by business owners before being treated as production policy.

- A **qualifying purchase** is a non-cancelled order with at least one sales-order line. All counts use distinct customer IDs.
- **Total customers** is the customer-directory population, including people with no purchase history. **Purchasing customers** counts directory IDs with qualifying history through the selected period end. **Customers in period** counts distinct buyers whose qualifying orders fall within the selected inclusive dates; this uses the same line-backed order scope as Executive Overview. Thus a directory/segment total of 1,000 and an Executive Overview period-buyer count of 857 can both be correct: they describe different populations, not a segment reconciliation error.
- **Active** and **inactive** are mutually exclusive observed-activity statuses across the directory as of the selected period end. Active means a last qualifying purchase on or after the 90-day cutoff; inactive means the last qualifying purchase is before that cutoff or no qualifying purchase exists. This is not a predicted churn label.
- Customer lifecycle distribution uses three mutually exclusive groups across the directory: **New** (first qualifying purchase in the selected period), **Returning** (purchased in-period and first purchased earlier), and **No purchase in selected period** (includes never-purchased directory records). These groups partition the directory. Revenue contribution is reported separately from customer count.
- Period net sales attributes order-line revenue to order date and subtracts refunds by return date within the selected period. The monthly trend attributes sales to order month and returns to return month. Lifetime customer net sales subtract recorded refunds through the selected period end. Source line revenue is before return adjustments; this is not an approved accounting definition of recognized revenue.
- Customer lifetime value is historical average lifetime net sales per purchaser with qualifying history, not a forecast of future value. Purchase frequency is qualifying orders divided by distinct period buyers. Segment average order value is period net sales divided by period orders; the customer-table lifetime average order value is lifetime net sales divided by lifetime orders.
- Cohorts include customers whose first qualifying purchase date is inside the selected dates, grouped by that first-purchase month. Month 0 is the acquisition month; a later month is retained only when the customer has a qualifying order in that month. Retention is retained distinct customers divided by the cohort size.
- RFM scores use deterministic quintiles through the selected period end for purchasers only: Recency 5 is most recent; Frequency 5 is most orders; Monetary 5 is highest lifetime net sales. Ties are ordered by customer ID. Customers with no purchase history are unscored. Mutually exclusive labels are assigned in this order: Champions (R≥4, F≥4, M≥4), Loyal (R≥3, F≥4), New (exactly one lifetime order and first purchase in the prior 30 days), Potential loyalists (R≥4, F≥2), At-risk (R≤2, F≥3), Needs attention (R≤2, F≤2), then Hibernating (remaining purchasers). These are descriptive heuristics, not machine-learning predictions or validated churn forecasts.
- KPI comparisons use the immediately preceding date interval with the same number of days as the selected inclusive period. Percentage change is unavailable when the comparison value is zero.
