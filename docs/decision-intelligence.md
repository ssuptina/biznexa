# Decision intelligence

BizNexa supports threshold-based decision signals, not automated actions. Signals are tied to a metric, a time window, and a clear business rule.

## What it does

- Compares current performance to a baseline window.
- Shows business rules such as inactivity, inventory thresholds, or supplier timing.
- Stores decision records with the metric, baseline, and review window.

## Limits

- These are review aids, not guaranteed predictions.
- They do not prove cause and effect.
- Business owners should approve definitions before using them in operations.
